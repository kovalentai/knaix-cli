Data Processing Addendum — Northwind Systems (Processor) and Client (Controller)

Section 2.1 Roles. For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Client acts as the Controller of Personal Data and Northwind Systems acts as the Processor. Northwind processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from Client, including with regard to transfers to a third country.

Section 3.2 Subprocessors. Client provides general authorization for Northwind to engage subprocessors. Northwind maintains a public list of current subprocessors and gives Client at least thirty (30) days' notice before adding or replacing a subprocessor, during which Client may object on reasonable data-protection grounds.

Section 4.1 Breach Notification. Northwind shall notify Client without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. The notification shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected, and the measures taken to address it.

Section 5.3 Data Residency. Personal Data submitted by Client is stored and processed in the geographic region selected by Client at provisioning time. Northwind shall not transfer Personal Data outside the selected region without Client's prior written consent, except as required to provide the support services expressly requested by Client.

Section 6.2 Data Subject Requests. Northwind shall, taking into account the nature of the processing, assist Client by appropriate technical and organizational measures in responding to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure, and portability, insofar as this is possible.

Section 7.4 Deletion and Return. Upon termination of the services, Northwind shall, at Client's choice, delete or return all Personal Data and delete existing copies within ninety (90) days, unless applicable law requires continued storage. A certificate of deletion is available upon written request.

Section 8.1 Audit Rights. Northwind shall make available to Client all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Addendum and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Client or an auditor mandated by Client, no more than once per twelve (12) month period, on at least thirty (30) days' notice.

Section 9.2 Security Measures. Northwind implements encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest, role-based access control, network isolation between tenants, and regular penetration testing. Encryption keys are managed in a dedicated key-management service and rotated at least annually.

Section 10.1 International Transfers. Where processing involves a transfer of Personal Data to a country without an adequacy decision, the parties rely on the applicable Standard Contractual Clauses, which are incorporated into this Addendum by reference and prevail in the event of a conflict.

Section 11.3 Liability. The liability of each party under this Addendum is subject to the limitations of liability agreed in the Master Services Agreement. Nothing in this Addendum limits any liability that cannot be limited under applicable data protection law.
