Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy — Effective January 1

1. Approval Thresholds. Expenses up to $500 may be approved by the employee's direct manager. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require director-level approval. Any single expense above $5,000 requires prior written approval from the Vice President of Finance before the expense is incurred.

2. Receipt Requirements. Itemized receipts are required for any expense of $25 or more. Expenses under $25 may be submitted without a receipt but must include a written description. Credit-card statements alone are not accepted as itemized receipts.

3. Meal Per Diem. The domestic meal per diem is $75 per day, allocated as $15 for breakfast, $20 for lunch, and $40 for dinner. Alcohol is not reimbursable under the per diem and must be excluded from all meal claims.

4. Mileage. Personal-vehicle mileage for business travel is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate of 67 cents per mile. Commuting between an employee's home and their regular office is not reimbursable.

5. Air Travel. Employees must book economy class for all flights under six (6) hours of scheduled flight time. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires prior VP approval regardless of flight length.

6. Lodging. Hotel room rates are reimbursable up to $250 per night in standard-cost cities and up to $400 per night in high-cost cities as defined in Appendix B. Room-service charges follow the meal per diem limits; in-room movies and minibar items are not reimbursable.

7. Submission Deadline. Expense reports must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted more than ninety (90) days after the expense date will not be reimbursed except with VP of Finance approval.

8. Non-Reimbursable Items. The following are never reimbursable: traffic and parking fines, personal entertainment, spouse or companion travel, loyalty-program fees, and lost or stolen personal property.

9. Foreign Currency. Expenses incurred in a foreign currency are converted to US dollars using the exchange rate on the transaction date as shown on the corporate card statement. Where no card was used, the published mid-market rate for the transaction date applies.

10. Corporate Card. The corporate card must be used for all reimbursable expenses where accepted. Personal cards may be used only where the corporate card is not accepted, and cash advances against the corporate card are prohibited.
